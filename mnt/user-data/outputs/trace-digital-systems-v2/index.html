Source: Federal Reserve, 2024 Small Business Credit Survey

When small businesses do apply for financing, lenders point to the same preventable culprit again and again: missing documents, numbers that don't match across statements, and applications that arrive incomplete. It's one of the most common — and most avoidable — reasons applications stall.

None of that is about whether the business is good. It's about whether the paperwork looks like it was built by someone a bank can trust to repay them.

Without organized books

  • Receipts scattered across texts, drawers, and memory
  • No clear answer when anyone asks "what did you actually make last quarter?"
  • Problems discovered at tax time, months too late to fix

With Trace

  • Every receipt logged the day it happens, over WhatsApp
  • A monthly snapshot that shows exactly where your money went
  • Issues flagged the month they happen — not months later, when it's too late to fix them
